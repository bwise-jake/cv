# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, and technical interviewers at senior web/growth engineering roles (e.g. Atlassian, Dovetail, similar product companies). They scan a resume in seconds before deciding whether to read closely. Some view it as a PDF export or print; some may open the HTML directly.

## Product Purpose

A personal CV for Jake Thornton-Bowen, positioned as a senior web engineer who bridges front-end engineering and growth/CRO. The document itself is part of the pitch: it should read as evidence of frontend craft and taste, not just list it. Success = a recruiter remembers the page looked sharp and found what they needed in under 10 seconds.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, confident, editorial-typographic. Closer to Linear / Stripe / Vercel marketing pages than a traditional HR resume template. Quietly technical rather than loud or playful.

## Anti-references

- Generic resume-builder templates (Canva-style): pill-shaped skill tags, rounded card-in-card boxes, stock icons, drop shadows on every element.
- Dense corporate-report look: heavy borders, boxed-in sections, grey-on-grey monotony.
- Anything that reads as "AI made this template."

## Design Principles

1. Typography carries the hierarchy, not boxes or color blocks.
2. Every visual choice should read as intentional craft, not decoration.
3. Scannability first: a recruiter should find role, company, and impact in one pass.
4. Restraint over ornamentation, one confident accent rather than a rainbow of tags.
5. Must survive both screen and print/PDF export without breaking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA color contrast. Real selectable/searchable text throughout (ATS-safe, no text-as-image). Must remain legible in black-and-white print.
